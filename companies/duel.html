WTF is Duel?

Duel is my answer to everything wrong with this industry — the rigging, the fake bets, the predatory bonus systems built to keep you hooked instead of treating you fair. I’ve been in this space a long time, and I got sick of watching operators squeeze every dollar out of their users through deceptive methods, not giving a fuck how shady the tactics were.

We keep our margins low for the love of the game. Most of the big casino names cap their best games around 99% RTP (return to player — the inverse of house edge). We’re already at 99.9%, and the goal is a true 100%.

That gap looks small on paper, but it isn’t: play for years at 99% RTP and your odds of ever being in profit are under 10%. At 99.9%, that jumps to somewhere in the 30–40% range. At a true 100%, it’s close to a coin flip — roughly 50/50. That’s before you even factor in the real-money policy: no fake bets, no manchild actors faking wins with house balance on camera. Whether someone’s betting one dollar or one million, it’s real.

Duel Originals

Duel Originals are Duel's own in-house games — not licensed from outside studios — covering formats like Dice, Crash, Plinko, Mines, and Blackjack. Each round is provably fair: the result is generated from a seed/hash pair players can independently verify after the bet settles, so outcomes can't be quietly altered.

Every Duel Originals game runs at 100% RTP — the game-by-game version of the zero-edge stance covered in the panel below.

Why zero edge beats a low edge

Most casinos run a 1–3% house edge, and that number compounds like interest — the more you play, the more certain the house's long-run win becomes. Duel's own published stance is that a true 0% edge keeps win/loss odds mathematically even no matter how much volume you put through it: no long-term drift in either direction, just variance. They're upfront about the tradeoff too — a genuinely zero-edge casino is expected to be unprofitable in the long run on that basis alone, which is why it's positioned as a deliberate bet, not a hidden gotcha.

Why no deposit bonuses

Duel's argument against traditional bonuses: what actually determines your expected loss is net edge — house edge minus whatever you get back in bonuses — and bonuses are almost always used to dress up a worse net edge behind wagering requirements, expiry windows, and game restrictions most players never fully read. Instead of that, Duel's stated approach is to skip bonus gimmicks and just publish a lower edge upfront.

What is Provably Fair?

Provably fair means you don’t have to trust us — you can prove it. We lock in every round’s outcome before you bet, and you can verify afterward that nothing was changed. That’s what keeps the game fair: not a promise, but something you can check yourself.
