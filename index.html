I am on a mission to build the fairest form of gambling, expose fraudulent operators & hunt down scammers.

How This Began

My journey into online entrepreneurship began with gambling in RuneScape. In 2014, that evolved into BigBoiBets, a gambling operation I co-founded with Kilian, using RuneScape gold instead of real currency. After I was betrayed and pushed out of the company, I decided to build something of my own.

Working day and night, I built a competing operation from the ground up in just a couple of weeks, only for Kilian’s team to have our host shut it down almost as quickly as it began.

I stepped away for a while, using the time to teach myself how to code and, more importantly, learn how to actually build and run a business. Then I watched Kilian scale BigBoiBets and expand into the CS scene with SteamPoker.com. Seeing that progression gave me the idea to build a competing platform of my own.

At eighteen, I scraped together a small loan and brought in an old friend as a co-founder. As the company grew, I eventually bought him out of the company, and continued building the business myself. That was the beginning of CSGOEmpire.

From the Empire to the Table

Poker had been part of my world long before I ever sat down at a live table. After years of building and running online gambling platforms, I eventually found my way into poker at the highest level. In 2024, Patrik Antonius, a fellow Finn and one of the game's biggest names, brought me in as his partner at a Triton event. Since then, I’ve gone on to play the biggest live high-stakes heads-up matches in the world.

— Monarch
